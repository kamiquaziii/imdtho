What a search fund is

Three steps, in plain language.

A search fund is a simple idea: back two operators to go find and run one good business, instead of starting from scratch or spreading bets thin.

01 — Raise

Investors back the operators

We raise a focused fund from people who’d rather bet on operators than on a spreadsheet. That capital funds the search and, ultimately, the acquisition.

02 — Search

We find the one

Full-time, we go looking for a single established business worth committing to — talking to owners directly, patiently, until we find the right fit.

03 — Run

We step in and operate

We buy it and take the wheel — not as absentee owners, but as the people showing up to run it, grow it, and steward what’s already working.

What we’re looking for

The kind of business we want to buy.

One company. Established, profitable, and worth running for a decade — not a turnaround and not a trend.

Durably profitable

Roughly $1M–$5M in EBITDA, with a track record of steady, healthy margins — not a bet on a turnaround.

Recurring, loyal revenue

Customers who come back — contracts, repeat business, or real switching costs — and no single account that makes or breaks the year.

An owner ready for the next chapter

A founder or owner thinking about succession, retirement, or simply stepping back — who cares where the business lands next.

A team worth protecting

People and a culture that already work. We’re buying to keep and build on that — not to strip it for parts.

Simple and durable

A business we can understand quickly and a market that isn’t going anywhere. We like boring-but-good.

Based in North America

Sector-agnostic across services, software, healthcare, and light manufacturing — we care more about quality than category.
